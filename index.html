How it works

For customers

  1. Share your location or type your suburb.
  2. Choose a category, like food or clothing.
  3. Call, WhatsApp or get directions to the closest business.

For businesses

  1. Create your account and profile with your address.
  2. Add products, prices and a promotion.
  3. Ask the advisor what to fix first to win more customers.

This is a working prototype. Profiles are saved in your own browser, so nothing is shared between devices until you connect a database.
